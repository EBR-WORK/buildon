English, Marathi and Hindi

Every page on the site reads in all three. A visitor picks a language once and the whole site follows — products, projects, blog posts, job openings, forms and all.

New content translates itself

You write in English. The Marathi and Hindi versions appear on their own, usually within minutes of publishing. Nothing to send to a translator, nothing to chase, nothing to pay per page.

Product names stay as product names

Bondit-151 and P-20 are printed on the bag, so they are left exactly as they are in every language. A dealer reading the Marathi page still recognises what to ask for.

Your own admin panel

Products, projects, blog posts, job openings, team members, branches and FAQs are all edited by you. No developer, no waiting, no change request.

Every enquiry reaches your sales team

An enquiry from the site becomes a lead in your CRM straight away, and can be assigned to a salesperson and followed up until it closes.

Day to day

What you can change yourself

The admin panel covers everything that changes about the business. If it is on the site, you can edit it.

Products

Add a product, change a description, swap a photo, retire an old bag.

Projects

Add a completed site with its photographs and the builder's name.

Blog

Write and publish articles, with headings, images and video.

Careers

Post a job opening; applications and résumés arrive in the panel.

Team

Add a colleague, update a photograph or a designation.

Branches

Keep the office and depot addresses current.

FAQs

Answer the question customers keep asking.

Home page

Change the banners and the sections on the front page.

Publishing takes one click

When you save, the site rebuilds and goes live on its own — including the Marathi and Hindi versions of whatever you just wrote. There is no separate step and nobody to ask.

Who can do what

Staff, admin and owner each see a different panel. Sensitive actions are owner-only, and a new colleague is invited by email rather than handed a password.

Enquiries

From the website to your sales team

Nothing is left sitting in an inbox waiting for somebody to notice it.

The enquiry arrives as a lead

A contact form or a product enquiry becomes a CRM lead the moment it is submitted, carrying the product, the quantity and the city the enquiry came from.

It gets an owner

Leads are assigned to a salesperson, who is notified. The panel shows what is assigned, what is being followed up and what has been closed.

Job applications, separately

Applications and their résumés arrive in their own section, so hiring does not get mixed up with sales.

Emails come from Buildon

Every automated email — a password reset, a lead notification — is sent in Buildon's name, with Buildon's wording. Nothing on it mentions the software underneath.

Being found

Built to be found and to stay up

The parts a visitor never sees, which decide whether they arrive at all.

A page for every city you sell in

Ranchi, Jamshedpur, Bhubaneswar, Cuttack, Durgapur, Siliguri, Jharkhand and Orissa each have their own page, so somebody searching for gypsum plaster in their own city can find you.

Search engines are told about all three languages

Google is told that the English, Marathi and Hindi pages are the same page in different languages, so the right one is shown to the right person.

The site is fast because there is nothing to wait for

Every page is prepared in advance rather than assembled when somebody asks for it. Pages open quickly, and there is no server that can go down under traffic.

Search inside the site

Visitors can search your products, projects and articles without leaving the page.

What it costs to run

Hosting and translation both run on free allowances that do not grow with your traffic. A busier site does not cost more.
